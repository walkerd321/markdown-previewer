# Privacy

Markdown Previewer is a local-first application:

- Markdown drafts remain in your browser's local storage.
- Imported and exported documents remain on your device.
- The hosted application does not receive draft content through a custom API.
- GitHub Pages and your browser/provider may process ordinary web requests and hosting logs.
- Theme, pane, and draft preferences are stored locally and can be removed by clearing this site's browser data.
